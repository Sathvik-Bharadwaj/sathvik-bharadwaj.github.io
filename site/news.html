03 · RESEARCH
MLCAD 2026

NetGen: agents that learn from failure.

Team circuitEvolve presented “NetGen: Failure-Aware Orchestrator Agents for Analog Netlist Generation” at the ACM/IEEE Symposium on Machine Learning for CAD. NetGen extends our work from tuning an existing circuit to generating the circuit itself: specialised agents coordinate on building an analog netlist, and a failed attempt is fed back as information rather than treated as a dead end.
