Our thesis

Digital design became software decades ago. Analog design never did. It is still tuned by hand, one simulation at a time, and it is still where silicon meets the real world.

We think the search through an analog design space should be done by computation, with engineers setting the goals and judging the evidence. That idea shapes everything we build.
